# 🤖 Intelligent Template Suggestion System

## ✨ **NEW FEATURE: AI-Powered Template & Subject Line Suggestions**

The CSOAF Email Campaign Manager now includes an intelligent template suggestion system that analyzes your targeting criteria and automatically recommends the most effective email templates and subject lines for maximum engagement.

---

## 🎯 **How It Works**

### **1. Smart Analysis**
The system analyzes your campaign targeting options:
- **Target Industries** (Technology, Healthcare, Financial Services, etc.)
- **Geographic Regions** (States and cities)
- **Sponsorship Levels** ($50K, $100K+, etc.)
- **Arts Interest Levels** (High, Medium, Low)
- **Campaign Description** (Keywords and themes)

### **2. Intelligent Recommendations**
Based on your selections, the AI suggests:
- **📋 Email Templates** - Optimized for your target audience
- **📧 Subject Lines** - Proven to increase open rates
- **💡 Email Approaches** - Strategic messaging guidance
- **🎯 Personalization Tips** - Industry-specific customization advice
